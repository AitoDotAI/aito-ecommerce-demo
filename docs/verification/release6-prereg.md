# Pre-registration: data release 6 (realistic showings, fictional brands, a large profile)

Written 2026-11-24, **before** any generator change, on Antti's decisions of 2026-11-23 (items 3, 4 and 8 of
`~/episto/ai/log/2026-11-23-ecommerce-decisions.md`; board ticket td-20261124130721630361). DRAFT: the band in section 2 is a
PROPOSAL for the CPO and Antti; nothing below is fixed until this file is merged. After the merge every threshold is fixed, and
any change is an amendment here first, with its reason.

## 0. What stays pinned

Release 5 is not touched. `data/` as committed (the files `data/release-env.json` counts, env `experiments-data`), the live demo
and every number quoted from it stay what they are. A new test lists the files the GENERATOR writes under `data/` (the tables
`data/release-env.json` counts and the answer key; not `data/precomputed/`, `data/agent_runs/`, `data/evidence/` or
`data/customer_profiles/`, which other steps and lanes write), records their sha256 and fails if any of them changes. The generator's default run (no new option) must still write those bytes (`R0` below).

Release 6 is written to its own directory, `data/releases/release6/`, by an explicit option (`--release 6`, with `--profile small`
or `--profile large`). The loaders, the data release gate and the `./do` commands take the release directory as an option too, so
release 6 is loadable and not only generated.

Only the MANIFEST is committed: `data/releases/release6/manifest.json` (per profile and table: row count and sha256 of the
generated file; the generator's git revision; the seed). The tables are not, because they are too large to commit as JSON.
`data/` is already 148 MB, and impressions take about 296 bytes per row. The small profile's impressions alone grow from 94,536
to roughly 270,000 to 1,070,000 rows with the sales held fixed (section 3), which is 80 to 320 MB. Both profiles are generated on
demand (`./do release6 --profile small|large`) into `data/releases/release6/<profile>/<table>.json` (git-ignored). The generator is
deterministic, so a profile is verified by regenerating it and comparing with the manifest (`M1`). The small profile's
regeneration is a slow test, run before a merge that touches the generator. The large profile is regenerated by hand.
Loading either profile into a shared env is Antti's step (ADR 0035); release 6 goes only to the local source-built engine until he
says otherwise.

## 2. The band (PROPOSED)

Measured on release 5, seed 42 (2026-11-24): 94,536 impressions, purchases per showing **0.227** overall; by placement
`buy_again` 0.825 (n 17,452), `checkout` 0.907 (n 1,221), `list` 0.079 (n 75,863). 21,457 purchased showings against 35,972 order
lines (the rest are `direct` sessions, ADR 0045). The two near-certain surfaces drive the overall rate: today a buy-again or
checkout showing is recorded almost only when it is bought.

Proposed, for the CPO and Antti to set, from the public sources below (searched 2026-11-24). Where a bound rests on an
assumption that no source states, the assumption is marked.

| # | source | what it measures | figure |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| S1 | Ma et al., *Entire Space Multi-Task Model* (ESMM), Alibaba, SIGIR 2018, https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.07931, Table 1 | impressions, clicks and purchases in Taobao's recommender feed | public set 84M impressions, 3.4M clicks, 18k purchases: **purchase per impression 0.00021**, click per impression 0.040, purchase per click 0.0053. | S3 | Coveo SIGIR eCom 2021 Data Challenge, https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.09423 | browsing sessions of a mid-sized shop | 10.43M product interactions, 77.8k purchases: **0.0075 per interaction**; 0.016 per session |
| S4 | Li et al., *A Next Basket Recommendation Reality Check*, ACM TOIS 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.14233 | a personal top-10 of earlier purchases (a buy-again strip) against the NEXT basket | precision@10 derived from the reported recall@10 × basket size ÷ 10: TaFeng 0.067, Dunnhumby 0.17, Instacart 0.31, GIVEN that an order follows |

| placement | band | from |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `list` (landing, category, search, for you, bought together) | 0.0005 to 0.005 | S1 per impression 0.0002 (low); S1's 4 % click × S2's 2.6 % purchase per view = 0.001 (central); the upper bound 5× the central for a small consumable-heavy shop is an ASSUMPTION |
| `buy_again` | 0.02 to 0.10 | S4's 0.17 to 0.31 for replenishment baskets × the chance the visit orders at all; that chance (5 to 30 % for a returning customer) is an ASSUMPTION; no source here gives it |
| `checkout` | 0.003 to 0.02 | S5: checkout 0.004 to cart offers 0.019 |
| all showings | 0.002 to 0.015 | the mix: e.g. 85 % list, 10 % buy again, 5 % checkout at the central rates gives 0.007 |

Release 5 is 16 to 160 times above the list band and far above the others, so F1 fails first there, as it must.

**OPEN, for the CPO: the cited band and mechanism A do not fit together as written.** Under A the 21,457 purchased showings stay,
so an overall rate of 0.002 to 0.015 needs 1.4 to 10.7 million showings in the SMALL profile (0.4 to 3 GB of JSON). The large
profile's 1,000,000 impressions with ~150,000 orders likewise mean that well under 5 % of its order lines can come from a logged
showing. Realistic data has exactly that shape: most of a pet shop's sales (reorders, direct visits) are not purchases of a
showing. So the proposal becomes **A3**: the sales stay byte-identical (S1 and S2 unchanged); a purchase that today is a
`buy_again` or `checkout` showing, and most `list` purchases, are recorded as purchases in a `direct` session (ADR 0045 allows
it), and only a SAMPLE of them stays a purchased showing. The sample is drawn from its own keyed stream and is stratified by
placement, so that, with the unbought showings added as in A, the small profile holds about 300,000 showings at the central
rates and the large one about 1,000,000. The cost is power: effects judged per showing then rest on roughly 2,000 purchased
showings in the small profile instead of 21,457, so S3 is judged with intervals, and an effect whose interval then spans its
release-5 value is reported as UNDERPOWERED rather than failed.

## 3. How C1 is planted (proposed: add the unbought showings, keep the sales)

Two ways were considered:

- **A (proposed).** Every session draws its sales exactly as today: the same streams, the same purchased showings, the same order
  lines. To that we ADD showings that were not bought:
  - the buy-again strip shows more of the household's earlier products than it buys (up to a fixed strip length);
  - the list is longer (later positions);
  - checkout shows more than the one dental chew.

  Each added showing draws its click from the same funnel as today (position, listing quality), from a NEW keyed stream
  (`stream(RNG_SEED, "unbought", ...)`), and it is never a purchase. So no existing draw moves, the sales are byte-identical
  (apart from the brands), and every planted effect judged on sales stays valid. The cost: an effect judged as a per-showing
  purchase rate is diluted, by the same factor for every product of a placement. Its direction and ratio hold; its absolute
  rate falls (`S3`).
- **B.** Lower the purchase odds and show more. The sales are redrawn, so every planted judgement of this repo and of aito-shopify
  must be re-run on seeds 42, 7 and 99. It is more realistic per showing and much more work. It is not proposed.

The strip length, list depth and checkout count are chosen on seed 42 ONLY, so that its rates fall in the middle of the band
(section 2). They are written here as an amendment, and only then is F1 run on the held-out seeds 7 and 99. Seed 42 is not a test
of F1, since the values were fitted on it.

## 5. How C3 is planted: the large profile

Sizes (Antti, 2026-11-23): about 10,000 products, 50,000 customers, 150,000 baskets (orders) and 1,000,000 impressions. More
products come from more variants per (brand, pet type, category) and more brands per tier: invented names, generated by the same
rule as section 4. Antti's option A: planted counts scale with the catalogue. A planted effect that names N products (incidents,
recalls, launches, price rises, offers) names N × (products ÷ 658), rounded, chosen by the same rule. Rates and multipliers are
unchanged. The large profile carries the brand mapping and C1's band too.

## 6. Checks

Every check runs on seeds 42, 7 and 99 unless it says otherwise. **Fail first:** F1 and B1 are run on release 5 before the
generator changes and must FAIL there (F1: 0.227 and 0.825 are outside the band; B1: release 5 names real brands). A check that
passes on release 5 checks nothing.
