## Aito quirks

- **Deletes (2.11.4):** a matching v2 `_delete` on a v1-engine table
  gives a 500 (td-20261009071333317983, fixed in 2.11.5). A delete that
  matches nothing gives 200.
- **Added columns:** a column added in place reports type `Unknown` and
  is modelled as categorical (td-20261012085637124211). New columns go
  in the initial schema.
- **Regression `why`:** with `model: "regression"`, `_estimate`'s why is
  exactly `exp(mean + Σ terms)`. The k-NN default's why can't be chained.

## Surprising

- **`/tmp` cleanup:** a cleaner removes files from long-lived scratch
  worktrees under `/tmp`. Commits are safe in `.git`, but unpushed
  working files are not.
- **Classifier vs allow rules:** auto mode's classifier blocked shared
  writes even with an allow rule in settings. An explicit "merge N" or
  "review, fix and merge" in chat is what let merges through.
