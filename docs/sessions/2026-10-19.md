## Aito findings

- **`_search … get` doesn't filter on the linked table's fields.** A
  `where` on `product_sku.*` only conditions `$p`. Shop search goes through
  `_recommend` instead.
- **Two-link `where` and `_relate` work on v2.** Both answered correctly for
  `product_state.product_sku.*` (2026-10-18).
- **Sparse, high-cardinality `_recommend` targets overconfide.** Product
  states with zero support scored 0.93 (td-20261018112625647694).
  `{clicked: false}` still tops at 0.32 after #1673
  (td-20261018112619845032).
