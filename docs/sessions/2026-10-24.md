## Aito quirks

- **Keys are per database on shared.aito.ai.** A key authenticates only
  under its own `/db/<name>/`, and a wrong key there answers a misleading
  405. So the sandbox had to be a database of its own.
- **v2 branches an env copy-on-write in milliseconds** (`POST _envs` with
  `basedOn: env.master`), addressed only by the URL path. An env idle for
  30 minutes is evicted from memory, and its next query is cold.
- **`_estimate` on `monthly_sales` rejects the linked
  `product_state.discount_band`** ("no such field"). So the units what-if
  follows the price only, and the view says so.
- **v2 writes go through `/data/_modify`.** Aito core measured 8 ms for an
  insert and 33–86 ms for an update on 2.11.8. Each band's lift in `$why`
  equals its observed purchase rate over the base rate.
