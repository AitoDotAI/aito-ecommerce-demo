# UCI Online Retail II, prepared (ADR 0034)

`outcomes.json` is the only file here that ships with the app. For every buyer with orders before
2011-09-01, it holds what they ordered next, and the Business reorders page marks the draft
against it. The training tables (`customers`, `invoices`, `lines`, `products`, `baskets`, about
47 MB) are regenerated by `./do uci-prepare` from the source xlsx. They're loaded into the Aito
env `uci` and kept out of git.

Source: Chen, D. (2012). Online Retail II [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D. Licence CC BY 4.0.
